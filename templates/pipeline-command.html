LinkedIn Cold InMail

First contact via LinkedIn — reference their project
Hi [NAME], I noticed [COMPANY] is working on [PROJECT] — are you currently working with a recruiter for that team? I specialise in placing Construction Managers, PMs, and Commercial Managers across SEQ and would love to share how I've helped similar firms build out their delivery teams.

Cold Email — Value First

Initial email — 3 sentences, no fluff
Subject: Quick question about [PROJECT] Hi [NAME], I'm a construction recruitment specialist focused on SEQ — I place Project Managers, Commercial Managers, and Construction Managers with developers and builders on the Gold Coast. Most of my clients come to me because a role opened up unexpectedly and they needed someone fast. Are you currently partnered with a recruiter for senior hires, or is that something you'd be open to chatting about? Chris

Cold Call Script

Phone intro — 30 seconds max
"Hi [NAME], it's Chris from Lead Group — I specialise in construction recruitment on the Gold Coast. Quick question — are you currently working with a recruiter for your senior hires, or is that something you handle in-house? I place PMs, CMs, and Commercial Managers with local developers and builders. If the timing's not right, no worries — happy to catch up in a few months."

Referral Opener

When you have a mutual connection
Hi [NAME], [MUTUAL CONNECTION] suggested I reach out — I'm Chris, a construction recruiter on the Gold Coast. I help developers and builders find exceptional PMs, CMs, and Commercial Managers across SEQ. Are you open to a quick 15-minute call this week?

Warm Follow-Up Email

Met at event or referred — keep momentum
Subject: Good to connect, [NAME] Hi [NAME], Good to meet you at [EVENT/REFERRA] — thanks for making time. As promised, here's a bit about how I work: I focus exclusively on construction recruitment across SEQ — placing PMs, CMs, Commercial Managers, and QSs with developers, builders, and civil contractors. Most of my clients come through referrals, and I pride myself on actually understanding the market rather than just sending CVs. If you're open to it, I'd love to grab a coffee (or a call) and learn more about your current or upcoming hiring needs. Best, Chris

Warm LinkedIn Message

After meeting at industry event or panel
Hi [NAME] — great chatting with you at [EVENT]. I'd love to learn more about what you're building at [COMPANY] and whether I might be able to help with your hiring. Are you free for a quick call this week?

Market Intel Nudge

Re-engage a warm contact with useful info
Subject: [PROJECT] update + quick question Hi [NAME], Saw [PROJECT] is progressing well — any updates on your team structure for the next phase? I came across [RELEVANT INTEL — e.g., "a PM who recently completed a similar-scale civil project on the GC is looking for their next move"]. Happy to share more details if it's useful — or if the timing isn't right, just say the word and I'll leave you alone for a bit! 😄 Chris

Project Completion Check-In

When a project is winding down — hiring signal
Subject: [PROJECT] — winding down? Hi [NAME], Hope the project is going well. I remember you mentioned [PROJECT DETAIL] — is the team starting to wind down ahead of completion? If you're expecting hiring needs in the new year / next phase, I'd love to be on your shortlist. I work exclusively in SEQ construction and have a strong network of PMs, CMs, and Commercial Managers who are ready to move. Chris

Role Introduction Email

When you have a strong candidate fit for their needs
Subject: [CANDIDATE NAME] for [ROLE] — quick intro? Hi [NAME], I have a [CANDIDATE TITLE] who I think could be a strong fit for [COMPANY]'s [PROJECT/TEAM]. [CANDIDATE NAME] has [X] years experience including [SPECIFIC RELEVANT PROJECT/SKILL]. They're currently [REASON THEY'RE LOOKING / OPEN TO MOVE]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week to discuss? Chris

Candidate Alert — Passive Tone

For hard-to-fill or senior roles
Subject: Came across someone worth knowing Hi [NAME], Not sure if you're hiring, but I came across [CANDIDATE NAME] — [BRIEF STRONG RELEVANT QUALIFICATION]. I thought of you given [REASON]. No pressure — just wanted to put them on your radar in case the timing is right. Happy to facilitate an intro if useful. Chris

Shortlist Submission

When submitting 2-3 candidates for a role
Subject: [X] candidates for [ROLE] at [COMPANY] Hi [NAME], Pleasure speaking with you yesterday. As discussed, here are [X] candidates for the [ROLE] position: 1. [NAME] — [TITLE] — [KEY STRENGTH] — [MOST RECENT ROLE] 2. [NAME] — [TITLE] — [KEY STRENGTH] — [MOST RECENT ROLE] Happy to arrange calls or provide full CVs. Let me know your thoughts. Chris

Contractor/Interim Pitch

When a client needs someone fast
Subject: Need someone for [ROLE] in the next 2-4 weeks? Hi [NAME], If you have an urgent gap on [PROJECT] and need someone who can hit the ground running — I know a few exceptional contractors in the SEQ market who could step in. Happy to send over profiles today if the timing helps. Chris

Follow-Up After No Response

5-7 days after initial outreach with no reply
Subject: Re: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT] — one more thing Hi [NAME], Just following up on my note from [DATE]. I know you're busy — this will be my last nudge for a while! Brief update: I placed a [ROLE] with [RELEVANT COMPANY/PROJECT] last week — another SEQ developer building out their delivery team. If the timing isn't right for a chat, no worries at all. I'll check back in a few months. Best, Chris

Call Follow-Up (Same Day)

After a phone conversation — next steps
Subject: Great chatting, [NAME] — next steps Hi [NAME], Really enjoyed our call today. As discussed: • I'll send over [X] candidate profiles by [DATE] • You'll review and get back to me by [DATE] • If they're a fit, we'll arrange client calls for [DATE] Looking forward to working together on this. Best, Chris

Post-Meeting Recap

After in-person meeting or coffee
Subject: Quick recap — [MEETING TOPIC] Hi [NAME], Great meeting you today. Quick recap of what we discussed: • [HIRING NEED 1] • [TIMELINE] • [NEXT STEPS] I'll be in touch with [X] by [DATE]. Don't hesitate to reach out if anything changes on your end. Thanks again for making time. Chris

Placement Follow-Up

After a successful placement — keep relationship warm
Subject: [CANDIDATE NAME] — 30-day check-in Hi [NAME], Hard to believe it's been a month since [CANDIDATE] started! How are they settling in? Always happy to stay in touch about future hiring needs — or if you know anyone in the industry who'd benefit from a conversation, I'd really appreciate the intro. Hope all is well with [PROJECT]. Chris
